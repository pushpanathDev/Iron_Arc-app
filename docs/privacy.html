Iron Arc Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026 · Applies to the Iron Arc app for Android and its servers

The short version. Iron Arc keeps what you need to log your training and get AI plans: your account, profile, workouts and plans. We don't show ads, we don't sell data, and the app has no tracking or analytics tools. The AI sees your training details but never your name or email. You can export your data or delete your account, and everything with it, from Profile at any time.

1. Who we are

Iron Arc is a workout logging and AI planning app made by an independent developer, Pushpanath ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy or your data: pushpanathmr@gmail.com.

3. How we use it

  • To run the app: your log, stats, reports, reminders and plans.
  • To create and adjust AI plans and coach replies for your training, equipment and the areas you go easy on.
  • To keep the coach safe: Safe mode after a "yes" on the health check, safety replies to danger signs, and reviewing reported replies.
  • To run Iron Arc Pro: your access, free trial and receipts.
  • To send emails about your Pro subscription (see section 6).
  • To prevent abuse, for example daily limits on AI use.

4. The AI coach and voice

To write plans and replies, the app sends Groq, our AI provider, what the AI needs: your training details (age, height and weight if given, goal, experience, equipment, training days, the areas you go easy on and your note), your recent workouts, your plan and your coach messages. It never sends your name, email or account IDs, and from the health check it shares only whether Safe mode is on, never your answers. We have turned on Groq's zero data retention setting for our account.

The coach is AI. It gives general fitness guidance, not medical advice, and it can be wrong. It's for adults (18+) only.

Speaking your sets and notes. While you speak, your phone's Google speech recognition shows your words as you say them (with no connection, the phone's offline recognizer does). It's part of Android and follows Google's privacy policy. When you finish, the recording goes to Groq to get the exact words, with the exercise's name so it's spelled right. Our server passes it straight on without saving it, Groq handles it under the same zero data retention setting, and the app deletes it from your phone as soon as the words are back.

5. Who processes your data

We use these service providers to run Iron Arc. They process data on our behalf, only to provide their service:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google): sign-in and email verification.
  • Supabase: our database (hosted in Singapore).
  • Render: the servers that run Iron Arc's backend.
  • Groq: the AI model behind plans and the coach, and the speech-to-text behind Speak your sets.
  • Resend: sends Iron Arc's emails.

We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't share it for advertising. We'd only disclose it if the law requires us to.

6. Emails

We send emails only about your Iron Arc Pro subscription: the welcome email with your receipt, a reminder two days before a trial turns into a paid plan, a confirmation when you cancel, and renewal receipts. No marketing emails. Firebase also sends sign-in and verification emails.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your data is kept while your account exists.
  • Deleting your account (Profile → Delete account) removes your data from our database right away (profile, workouts, plans, chats, ratings, reports, subscription and receipts) and deletes your sign-in.
  • One thing stays: if you had a free trial, its email fingerprint (section 2) is kept so a new account with the same email doesn't get another trial. It can't be turned back into your email.
  • Our providers' backups may hold copies for a limited time before they are overwritten.

8. Your choices and rights

  • See and change your profile and settings in the app at any time.
  • Export your workouts and body weight as CSV or PDF from Profile.
  • Delete your account and data from Profile.
  • Redo the health check or turn Safe mode on or off from Profile → Health check.
  • Turn off reminders in Profile → Notifications.

Depending on where you live, including India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have further rights, such as asking for a copy of your data, correcting it, or raising a complaint. Contact us at pushpanathmr@gmail.com and we'll respond.

9. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to our database and servers is restricted, and the AI only ever receives the training details it needs. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

10. Children

Iron Arc is for people 13 and over. The AI coach and Iron Arc Pro are for adults (18+). If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
